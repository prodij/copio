Copio

A standalone SaaS boilerplate in prodij/copio. Every product is a copy of it plus its own business logic, written fresh from intent notes rather than ported code.

1 · One product at runtime

Everything a product needs lives in its own repo and its own AWS account. The backend is the only place that decides who may do what; Zitadel and Lago run as unmodified images.

2 · The repeatable pattern

The boilerplate supplies everything generic. Business logic never moves as code: it is extracted as intent into the brain, then implemented fresh in the new stack. Fixes made in a product flow back into the boilerplate so the next product starts better.

Decided

Design so far

  • Personal project, separate from NG and Skylit
  • Each product stands alone: one repo, one AWS account, no shared platform
  • Repo layout: frontend/ backend/ jobs/ infra/, deployed separately by folder
  • Stack: React SPA, FastAPI, Postgres, Terraform
  • Customer sign-in: Zitadel per product (branding)
  • Billing: Lago, unmodified; payments by Stripe
  • Jobs: Lambda + EventBridge, raw data to S3 first
  • Products made with Copier; template lives in prodij/copio
